02 · Importation

Your exact spec, delivered to Nairobi.

Japan, the UK, the UAE or Europe. We handle the whole chain, from finding the car to putting Kenyan plates on it.

  1. 01

    Source

    We search verified dealers and auctions for your make, trim, colour and mileage.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    Independent pre-shipment inspection and full history check before any money moves.

  3. 03

    Ship

    Roll-on/roll-off or container shipping to Mombasa, insured end to end.

  4. 04

    Clear

    KRA duty, KEBS compliance and port clearance, handled by our agents.

  5. 05

    Register

    NTSA registration, Kenyan plates and logbook in your name.

  6. 06

    Hand over

    Detailed, fuelled and handed over at the showroom or at your door.
